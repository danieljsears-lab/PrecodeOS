Why it exists

The Gap

The first mile is getting easier. The second mile is where things drift.

Code generation is becoming cheap and fast. Human judgment, verification, direction, and reliable recovery are becoming the scarce controls.

Where drift begins

Without an operating model, speed hides the cost of uncertainty.

A conversation can produce momentum without leaving a durable record of what the project is trying to achieve.

Invisible model-made additions can turn an approved idea into a much larger change before anyone has reviewed the boundary.

A confident answer from an LLM is not proof that the software works, is secure, or remains maintainable.

When state, scope, proof, or direction becomes unclear, builders need a route back to inspection and repair.

Precode gives the project a durable structure for moving from an idea to a reviewed, maintainable slice of software.
