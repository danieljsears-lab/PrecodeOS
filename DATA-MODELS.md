## Risk-Triggered Production-Readiness Guidance

Use this section only when an approved PRD identifies schema, migration, retention, audit-history, or destructive-data risk. Record entities and relationships, source of truth, lifecycle and retention rules, migration or backfill assumptions, rollback or blocked escape, and verification evidence. Do not add speculative fields, duplicate PRD behavior, or treat a documented schema as proof of production readiness.
